## Share your result

1. Open a [community result](https://github.com/KajetanMuczynski/quadami/issues/new?template=community-result.yml)
   issue on GitHub (you need a free account). The form asks for the images, the panel's build line,
   how you want to be credited, the license, and your permission.
2. A maintainer replies there and adds your entry. The issue stays as the written record of your
   permission.

What we need from you, and what you should know before you send it:

- **Your own work.** You made the model, or you have the right to share it. No models of real
  people, and nothing made from someone else's model without their permission.
- **A license for the images.** CC BY 4.0 (you are credited) or CC0 (no conditions). The images are
  stored in the project's public Git repository and shown on this site.
- **Withdrawing.** Ask in the same issue and the entry is removed from the site at its next update.
  Copies already in the Git history, on mirrors or in someone's download stay, as with anything
  published on the web; send only what you are fine with staying public.
- **Bug reports** go to the [bug report form](https://github.com/KajetanMuczynski/quadami/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) instead; nothing from
  a bug report is shown here.

Permission given another way counts only in writing, for example by e-mail to
[quadami.contact@gmail.com](mailto:quadami.contact@gmail.com); the entry then says
"Permission: by e-mail, <date>" and the maintainer keeps the message.
